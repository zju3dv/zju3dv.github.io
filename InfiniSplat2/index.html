TL;DR

  • InfiniSplat v2 reconstructs 3D Gaussian scenes from arbitrary input views, with or without camera poses.
  • A dual-branch architecture freezes geometry and learns Gaussian attributes, with opacity suppressing artifacts from inconsistent points.
  • Training with scale-aligned ground-truth cameras and large-angle target views improves rendering under large viewpoint changes.
