TL;DR

ScaleNav learns navigation priors from Internet egocentric videos through action-free pretraining and transfers them to action generation for language-conditioned local navigation.

Demonstrations

Target highlights and “Arrival” labels are presentation annotations in the edited demos.

From video to action.

ScaleNav transfers navigation priors learned from Internet egocentric videos to action generation through action alignment and joint fine-tuning on simulated robot trajectories.

The task is to reach a language-specified target visible in the initial view and stop at the instructed location.

One model. Three stages.

A shared Diffusion Transformer jointly predicts future video latents and action chunks from the instruction, current RGB observation, and memory.

01

Video pretraining

Learn goal-directed visual dynamics from action-free Internet egocentric videos paired with navigation instructions.

02

Action alignment

Train the action modules and shared self-attention layers on action-labeled simulated trajectories.

03

Joint fine-tuning

Fine-tune the shared backbone and both output heads, continuing Internet video replay from action alignment.
