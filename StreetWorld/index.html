TL;DR

StreetWorld is a high-fidelity benchmark for fully closed-loop autonomous driving, where policy actions shape subsequent observations.

It provides 2,000+ scenes, combining dynamic Gaussian splatting reconstructions from and Waymo with integrated NuRec assets.

A Gym-style interface and modular design let researchers customize evaluation settings and integrate new scenes, rendering backends, and driving policies.

Street Gallery

StreetWorld provides over 2,000 photorealistic, dynamic street scenes, combining reconstructions from Waymo and nuScenes with integrated NuRec assets. This gallery showcases representative scenes spanning different weather, road conditions, and driving behaviors.

Framework

StreetWorld consists of three core modules: the Scene Manager, the closed-loop simulation framework, and the Env Interface. The Scene Manager initializes scenarios by loading and parsing their assets and supports scene editing.

During simulation, Actor and Participants manage the ego agent and other traffic entities, respectively. Each agent perceives the Environment through its Observer, selects actions through its Policy, and executes those actions through its Controller. The Environment collects actions from all agents, advances the physics simulation, and computes rewards using a configurable evaluation and reward model.

During evaluation, different AD policies interact with the simulator through the unified Env Interface, either remotely or locally.
